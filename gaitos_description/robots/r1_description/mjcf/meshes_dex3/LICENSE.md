# R1 Dex3 meshes

Derived from the meshes of the Unitree R1 (unitree_mujoco) and of the Dex3-1 hand of the G1
(unitree_ros), BSD-3-Clause, Copyright (c) Unitree Robotics. Each `<link>_<colour>.STL` is a
part of the stock visual mesh of that link, cut by material for the stock R1 colours;
`*_wrist_roll_link.STL` is the wrist clipped where the stock fist starts.
Installed into `mjcf/meshes/` next to the Unitree meshes that `r1_dex3.xml` also uses.
